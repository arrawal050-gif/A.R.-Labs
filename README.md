# A.R. Labs — Creative Engineering Hub & Ecosystem

> **Applied Intelligence, Embedded Systems & Open Architectures**

Official creative engineering hub and unified ecosystem showcase for **A.R. Labs**. Engineered with a high-contrast minimalist dark aesthetic, Lenis smooth scrolling, GSAP interactive timelines, and SVG liquid displacement.

---

## 🚀 Core Digital Platforms (Web Apps)

- **[AI-OS Platform](https://ai-os-powerd-by-ar-labs.vercel.app/)** — Unified operating environment for autonomous AI workflows, multi-agent toolkits, and ecosystem growth.
- **[A.R. Publications](https://arproduction050-byte.github.io/A.R.-Publications/)** — Creative and research publication division housing technical articles, literature, philosophical explorations, and educational reference treatises.
- **[Help Desk EEE](https://help-desk-eee.vercel.app/)** — Departmental support hub and academic utility platform for electrical and electronics engineering workflows with 360° virtual laboratory panoramas.

---

## 🔬 Hardware & Research Initiatives

- **Sakhi Hardware Companion (Early Childhood Edition)** — Screenless, low-distraction embodied voice companion for children aged 3–5 years old. Powered by an ESP32 edge microcontroller, I2S DAC (MAX98357A), full-duplex WebSockets, Google Gemini intelligence, Piper neural voice, and ChromaDB story vector caching.
- **BlackBox — Mental Health Stabilization Platform** — Intelligent cognitive telemetry and grounding platform designed to monitor daily mood shifts, offer cognitive behavioral support, and build personal resilience through passive telemetry and edge privacy.

---

## 📱 Native Android Utilities (.APK Distribution)

- **Bluetooth Extender Utility (`v1.2.0`)** — Low-level audio routing kernel, extended packet range repeater mode, and peripheral handshake monitor (`17.9 MB`).
- **Academic & Communication Systems Portal (`v2.0.4`)** — Offline DSP audio lecture synchronization, 8086 microprocessor simulation with virtual registers, and unit-by-unit exam question banks (`6.5 MB`).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** React 19 + Vite 8
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (v4)
- **Smooth Scrolling:** Lenis
- **Animations:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger
- **Visual Filters:** SVG `<filter id="liquid-filter">` (`feTurbulence` & `feDisplacementMap`)
- **Typography:** Bebas Neue, Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono
- **Icons:** Lucide React
